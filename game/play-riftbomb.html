Blast doctrine

  • Bombs erupt in four straight lanes. Stone blocks stop every blast.
  • Break Hextech crates to uncover extra bombs, blast range, movement speed, and shields.
  • Power-up layouts are mirrored every round, so both corners receive equal opportunities.
  • One blast can trigger another bomb. Every explosion—including your own—can eliminate you.
  • Win three rounds before the rival. A double knockout replays the round without a point.

Classic arena contract

Permanent stone pillars never break. Crates stop a blast and disappear after one hit. The bomber who plants a bomb may leave its tile once; stepping back into it is blocked.

Katarina’s assassination route

Q leaves a dagger behind its target. F drops another dagger and grants movement speed. E blinks to the nearest dagger or rival; picking up that dagger triggers Voracity. R only channels when the rival is close enough, and movement stops during the blade storm.

Zed’s forbidden technique

Q and E are copied by every active shadow. F throws a Living Shadow into a free arena cell; press F again before it fades to swap positions. R appears behind the rival, leaves a death shadow at the cast point, and detonates its mark after a short execution window.

Renekton’s fury route

Q carves a circle and heals from targets hit. F lunges into a brutal stun. E slices forward and grants a second dash after contact. At 50 Fury, Q or F consumes it for an empowered cast; R activates Dominus, enlarges Renekton and burns nearby rivals.

Vladimir’s crimson cycle

Q drains a target and heals Vladimir, with every third cast empowered. F becomes an untargetable blood pool, E releases a radial blood burst at the cost of health, and R marks an area before detonating for amplified damage and a large heal.
