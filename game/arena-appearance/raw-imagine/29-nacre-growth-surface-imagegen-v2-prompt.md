# Nacre growth surface — Imagegen v2

Production-ready square seamless material for the Nacre Hollow breakable
growths. Generated as a surface asset rather than an arena screenshot.

- Orthographic albedo-only material, no board, camera, text or isolated prop.
- Large rounded oyster-pearl lobes and overlapping shell folds.
- Warm ivory/champagne base with restrained rose, lilac and cyan iridescence.
- Small turquoise mineral seams and a bright mid-high value range.
- Edge-to-edge tiling for triplanar mapping on the procedural growth mesh.
- Avoids the charcoal reef, tiny noisy barnacles and baked directional light of
  the rejected v1 material.
